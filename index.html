Weather Analysis in Australia

Average Rainfall Map

This map visualizes the average rainfall distribution across various regions in Australia. Using the Mercator projection, it shows the areas with different levels of rainfall, where the colors represent the intensity of rainfall. The map is color-coded in shades of blue, with darker blue indicating higher average rainfall and lighter blue showing areas with lower rainfall.

The visualization highlights regional disparities in rainfall across Australia, helping to distinguish wetter areas (like coastal regions) from drier, inland areas.

Max Temperature Over Time

This chart visualizes maximum temperature trends over time across selected Australian cities such as Albury, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth. Users can observe the pattern of temperature spikes during summer and cooler temperatures in winter.

Rainfall Over Time

This graph shows the rainfall trends over time for selected Australian cities. The y-axis is constrained to a maximum of 200mm for readability. The visualization highlights the fluctuations in rainfall across different Australian regions, helping to understand seasonal and regional variations in precipitation, which is essential for water management, agriculture, and climate studies.
